   - Maths P2: Section B was 12/55; Q11 (19) and Q12 (20) blank. Teacher: "Too little attempted. This is 50% of the paper."

   - Fix: do part (a) of every Section B question first; move on after ~2 min stuck; use given "show that" results in later parts; aim ~1 min per mark.
2. **Writing too much / outside the box.** About 7 Chem marks were lost as "not marked" because answers ran outside the box. Mark schemes reward roughly 8-word keyword answers.
3. **Uncertainty rules, shared by Physics 1B and Chem 1B** (~10 marks across both):
   - Two readings (burette, calliper zero) → double the uncertainty.
   - Powers multiply the % uncertainty (√ → ½, cube → 3×).
   - Absolute uncertainty to 1 s.f.
   - Improve precision by repeating or measuring many and dividing, not by "use a better instrument".
   - Check units (kW vs kWh, cm³ not cm⁻³).
4. **Not using the GDC in Maths P2.** Q10(b) was done in about 15 lines by hand; Q11 (probability density function) is almost entirely GDC work.
5. **No sanity check on absurd numbers.** $9,649,000 from $100k at 1.2%; 36,000 m/s gas molecule speed.
6. **Forcing "show that" answers.** Maths P1 Q11(a): got sin⁻¹(½) = 30° and then wrote 60°.
